SFX Travel Manager

One connected workspace for travel bookings, vehicle assignments, driver duties, invoices, and payments.

All selected work

The problem

A trip passes through several people and records: a booking, a vehicle assignment, a driver’s duty, a customer invoice, and a payment. Managing these separately creates repeated entry and makes follow-up harder.

What SFX built

A product that connects those workflows for Indian travel and taxi operators. The public product includes bookings, customer booking links, a driver experience, GST invoices, khata, and reporting.

A complete workflow

  1. Capture a booking and the passenger’s trip details.
  2. Assign a vehicle and driver.
  3. Collect duty details from the driver.
  4. Prepare the invoice and record the payment.
  5. Review outstanding balances and operational reports.

What it demonstrates

A product designed around linked business records, different user roles, and a complete operational journey. The live demo and help centre make the workflow available to inspect.

Scope of this example

This is an SFX-owned software product. We present it as business software experience, not as a Salesforce implementation or an external client engagement. Time-saving figures on its product page are labelled estimates rather than measured customer outcomes.
