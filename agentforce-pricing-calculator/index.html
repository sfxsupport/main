Your real bill is probably higher

List price hides four things:

  • Discounts and minimum purchases on your contract.
  • Data 360 credits for indexing and retrieval, unless you enter them.
  • Extra prompt or AI usage billed on top, such as AI calls inside a custom action.
  • Help Agent, which is billed per resolved case, login licences, and actions that retry or loop.

Four ways to spend less

  1. Merge small lookups. One Apex or Flow action that returns everything beats three tiny ones. Every action is billed.
  2. Keep topics narrow. Fewer, well-described actions stop the planner calling ones it doesn't need.
  3. Count in a sandbox first. Run real customer questions and count actions per chat before you pick a model.
  4. Check Digital Wallet weekly in your first month. It's in the App Launcher in production.

Questions people ask

How much does one Agentforce action cost?

20 Flex Credits in production (16 in sandbox), 30 for voice. At $500 per 100,000 credits that's $0.10 per text action and $0.15 per voice action.

Is Flex Credits or $2 per conversation cheaper?

They match at 20 text actions per conversation. Fewer actions, Flex Credits cost less; more, the $2 price wins. Included credits and discounts move the line.

Can I use Flex Credits and per-conversation pricing in the same org?

No. Salesforce's pricing FAQ says they aren't supported in the same org, so you choose one.

What does the Agentforce per-user add-on cost?

$150 per user per month, billed yearly, for Sales, Service and Field Service; $175 for Industries. Max Edition starts at $550. Extra prompt usage can be billed on top depending on your contract.

Does Data Cloud grounding cost extra?

It can. Data Library indexing and retrieval use credits from the Data Services or Flex Credits card, depending on your contract.

Do Enterprise orgs get free Flex Credits?

Salesforce Help says Enterprise Edition and above can get 100,000 Flex Credits with Salesforce Foundations, and some editions list more. Check your order form.
