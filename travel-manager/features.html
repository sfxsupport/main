Bookings

A booking in two minutes, not twelve.

One form for the passenger, trip, car and advance. One save creates the booking, its duty slip and the receipt.

  • Returning passengers picked, never typed again
  • Your rate appears when you choose the car type
  • Free cars and drivers in green, so nothing is double booked
  • Weddings and events: many cars on one booking

Booking link

Customers book themselves, day or night.

Your own page with your logo, Call and WhatsApp. Three screens on the phone, no app and no login. You accept with one tap.

  • Pickup and drop from the map, stops and night stays
  • Requests arrive with a red count, and by email or WhatsApp
  • Spam stopped by a human check and limits per number
  • Share the link or its QR code anywhere

Duty slips

Duty slips that fill themselves.

Every car on every day in one list. The driver's phone adds the km, toll, fuel and the passenger's signature, with photos.

  • Need a vehicle, Running, Upcoming, To check
  • Send the slip to driver and passenger on WhatsApp
  • Odometer photos, and where each trip started and ended
  • The invoice completes the slips by itself

Driver app

Your drivers, on their own phone.

Each driver signs in with mobile and PIN, and sees only their trips, leave and salary. Never fares, invoices or khata.

  • Start trip with the odometer photo, end with a signature
  • Toll, parking, fuel and repairs with receipt photos
  • Leave requests and this month's salary
  • On duty now: who is on the road, on your dashboard

GST & khata

Bills and khata, right to the paisa.

The GST invoice is made from the trip, with the advance taken off. Every agent's and transporter's khata updates by itself.

  • CGST + SGST or IGST, with the customer's GSTIN
  • On your letterhead, shared as a PDF
  • Payments clear the oldest bills first
  • Khata statements in PDF or Excel, safe to send

  • Fuel fills and km per litre for every car
  • Service and repairs, from the office or the driver's phone
  • Trips and revenue by car
