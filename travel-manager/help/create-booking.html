Create a booking

Book a trip for a customer: who travels, when, from where to where, and which car. One save makes the booking and its duty slip.

Owner, Booking staffWebsiteAbout 3 min11 steps
  1. 1

    On the left menu, click Bookings.

  2. 2

    Click + New booking.

  3. 3

    In Agent/Vendor, choose who the booking is for. For your own customer, choose your company (it says my account). For an agent's trip, choose the agent.

  4. 4

    Click New passenger, then type the First name, Last name and Mobile (10 digits). A passenger you had before? Click Existing and pick them instead.

  5. 5

    Under 2 · Trip, choose the Duty type (Local, Outstation, Airport…) and the City. Set the Start date, the End date for a trip of several days, and the Reporting time.

  6. 6

    Type the Pickup and Drop address. Search Google finds a place on the map; Enter manually lets you type it.

  7. 7

    Under 3 · Vehicle, choose the car type in Vehicle category. Your rate for it shows by itself.

  8. 8

    Optional: pick the Vehicle and Driver now. Free ones have a green dot, busy ones are marked Booked. Or leave Assign later and give them on the duty slip.

  9. 9

    Under 4 · Other details: where the booking came from, and if the customer paid an advance, the Booking amount received now and Paid by. A receipt is made for it.

  10. 10

    Click Create booking.

  11. Done. The booking opens with its number, the duty slip for the car, and the advance receipt. Its status is Confirmed; it becomes Vehicle Assigned once a car and driver are given.

    12. Something wrong in red? Nothing was saved yet: fix that box and click Create booking again.

    Many cars for a wedding? In 2 · Trip click Multi vehicle (many cars) and add one row per car type with the number of cars.
