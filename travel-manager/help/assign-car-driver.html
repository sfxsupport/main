Give a car and driver to a trip

Every car on a booking has a duty slip. Put the car and the driver on it, then send the driver the trip on WhatsApp.

Owner, Booking staffWebsiteAbout 2 min6 steps
  1. 1

    Open the booking. In the Duty slips box, click the duty slip (DS-…).

  2. 2

    Click Assign vehicle.

  3. 3

    Pick Our vehicle. Free cars have a green dot; a car already on another trip shows Booked.

  4. 4

    Pick Our driver the same way. Drivers on leave are shown in amber.

  5. 5

    Click Save.

  6. 6

    The duty slip is now Assigned. Click Send to driver (WhatsApp): WhatsApp opens with the trip details typed; just press send. Send to passenger does the same for the customer.

    7. Using a transporter's car? In the same box choose Given to Agent/Vendor (transporter) and type their car number, driver and mobile.

    The driver now sees this trip in the phone app under My duties. See Driver: do a trip on the phone.
