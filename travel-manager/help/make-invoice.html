Help Centre/Money

Make the invoice for a trip

Bill the trip with GST. The advance already paid is taken off by itself, and the khata updates at once.

Owner, AccountsWebsiteAbout 3 min8 steps
  1. 1

    Open the booking and click Generate invoice.

  2. 2

    Choose how to bill: Enter manually (km, rate, toll…) or Fixed amount (one agreed price).

  3. 3

    Check the boxes. Actual km comes from the duty slip; the rate from your rate card. Add Toll, Parking, Night charge or taxes if any.

  4. 4

    Under GST, choose the GST rate (No GST, 5% or 18%) and the GST type: CGST + SGST inside your state, IGST for another state. For a company bill, choose Enter new GST details and type their GSTIN.

  5. 5

    Click Preview and read the bill: each line, the GST, the booking amount taken off, and the balance to pay.

  6. 6

    All correct? Click Create invoice.

  7. Done. The invoice opens. Print / PDF prints it on your letterhead with logo and signature; Send… sends it by WhatsApp or email.

  8. 8

    This is how the printed tax invoice looks.

    9. The booking is now Invoiced and its open duty slips are completed. To change a figure later use Edit lines; to undo, Cancel the invoice with a reason (invoices are never deleted).
