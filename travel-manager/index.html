Questions

Frequently asked

Who is SFX Travel Manager for?

It is designed for travel agencies, taxi operators and fleet owners working with direct customers, corporate clients, Agents/Vendors, attached vehicles and drivers.

Does the customer need an account to fill the booking link?

No. The secure link opens directly on the customer's phone. They see your agency branding and only the fields needed for their trip.

Does a customer submission automatically confirm the booking?

No. The information returns to the Draft booking. Your office reviews the trip, checks availability and confirms it.

Can it manage GST invoices and monthly CA reporting?

Yes. It supports GST invoices and downloadable GST summary and invoice reports that can be shared with your CA.

Can drivers see our rates and khata?

No. The driver view is restricted to their duties, route and trip actions. Financial information stays with authorised office users.

Can we manage both owned and attached vehicles?

Yes. The fleet area supports your own vehicles and vehicles supplied by other operators, along with assignment, driver and operating records.
