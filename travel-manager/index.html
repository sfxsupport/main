Is it for taxi operators, travel agents or tour operators?

All three. It handles your own fleet, attached vehicles, duties received from agents and duties given to other operators, with the khata for each party.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in the browser on any computer, and as an app on Android and iPhone. Your team just signs in.

Can my drivers use it without seeing our prices?

Yes. Drivers sign in with their mobile number and a PIN and see only their own duties: no fares, invoices or khata.

Does it make GST invoices?

Yes. Tax invoices with CGST + SGST or IGST, your GSTIN, SAC code and the amount in words, plus booking amount receipts and payment vouchers.

I use Excel or another software today. Can I move my data?

Yes. We bring in your Agent/Vendors, passengers, vehicles and open balances, so you start with your khata as it is today.

How does the AI assistant work, and is it safe?

You connect SFX to Claude or ChatGPT once with a private address. It sees only your company, follows the same rules as the website, asks before it changes anything, and every action is in the activity log. Switch it off in one click.

Is my data safe? Can I take it out?

Backups every hour, kept encrypted off the server and tested every night. Your data is yours: export reports and statements to Excel any time.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at ₹200 a month and depend on the number of users. No setup fee. We suggest the right plan on the free demo.

Do you give training and support?

Yes. We set it up with you, train your team, and help on call and WhatsApp in Hindi and English.
