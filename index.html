Who is behind SFX Support?+

SFX Support is a Salesforce + AI solutions team led by founder Abhay Nama, who reviews every piece of work. We publish solution guides checked against official docs, and build Agentforce, Prompt Builder and AI integrations for clients.

What does SFX work on?+

Salesforce + AI: Agentforce agents and custom actions, Prompt Builder and the Models API, Claude, OpenAI and Gemini integrations from Apex, RAG and knowledge assistants, AI workflow automation, and fixing AI that is already live. We also fix Apex, Flow, LWC and integration issues.

How much does a project cost?+

Every project gets a written scope and a fixed quote before work starts, so you see the price, timeline and exclusions first. Model usage is billed by the AI vendor to your own account.

How does Ask Krrish work?+

Ask Krrish is an AI assistant for Salesforce questions: code help, best practices and debugging guidance. Sign in with Google to start. There is a daily message limit that resets every 24 hours.

Is SFX affiliated with Salesforce?+

No. SFX Support is independent. We are not a Salesforce partner and are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Salesforce, Inc.
