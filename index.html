well-defined problem.

Share the business need, the current behaviour, and your constraints. We identify what information is needed to scope the work.

Agree a bounded scope

Build, test, and review

Work in an agreed development environment. Review the change against the requirements and test relevant failure cases.

Deploy and hand over

Agree the release, share the changes and operating notes, and define any follow-up support in the proposal.
