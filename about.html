How we work

Rules we don't bend.

01

Written scope, fixed quote

What we'll do, what we won't, the timeline and the price, in writing. Nothing starts until you agree.

02

Built and tested in your sandbox

We work in a sandbox, test against the scenarios in the scope and show you the result before anything goes to production.

03

AI makes us faster. A developer stays responsible.

We use AI to read errors and draft code. A developer reviews and tests every change before you see it.

04

The code is yours

You get the code, the tests and short handover notes on what changed and why.

05

Checked against official docs

Before a guide is published, two AI reviewers (Claude and ChatGPT) check it against official Salesforce and vendor docs, and a person approves it.

06

No invented numbers

We don't publish made-up savings, rankings or client counts. If we can't show it, we don't claim it.
