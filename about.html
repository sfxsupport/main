Our Mission

Here when it
matters most.

Whether you're a developer trying to understand governor limits, an admin learning automation, or someone prepping for a Salesforce interview — SFX is a place to learn, explore, and grow alongside the community.

"Good resources and the right tools can make a huge difference — that's all SFX is trying to be."

Non-Commercial

SFX is not a business, consultancy, or agency. No hidden agenda — just genuine help when you need it.

Always Available

Guides, tools, and AI available around the clock — learn at your own pace, whenever you need it.

Built on Trust

We're not here to sell. You choose how to engage, how much to share, and if you ever want to give back.

Process

What SFX
offers.

Three things, completely free.

01

Learning Guides

Deep, practical guides on Apex, LWC, SOQL, Flows, integrations, deployment, and more — written for practitioners, covering real concepts not just exam topics.

02

Krrish AI — Salesforce AI

Ask anything Salesforce — code, debugging, best practices, interview prep. The AI responds instantly, free for the community with a daily message limit.

03

Debug Log Analyzer

A free browser tool to capture, read, and analyze Salesforce Apex debug logs with AI — spot governor limit issues, N+1 SOQL, and field-level traces in seconds.

Our Ethos

A quiet
movement.

SFX is a personal side project — built quietly, shared openly. No team, no org, just one person who wanted to give something back to the Salesforce community.

Hi, I'm Krrish — a Salesforce Technical Support Engineer. I built SFX in my personal time because I kept running into the same gaps: guides that assumed too much, no quick AI to ask, and debug logs that were impossible to read. SFX is my attempt to fix that — one resource at a time.

No logos. No upsells. Just help. And if this support makes a difference in your journey — we hope you'll pay it forward.
