Frequently asked questions

If your question is not here, message the founders directly. You will not be sent through a sales call center.

founders@sfxsupport.com
Is SFX Grove another recorded Salesforce course?+

No. Grove is structured around guided missions, project work and reviewable evidence. The aim is to help you explain and demonstrate what you build, not only watch lessons.

Is there an application or training fee?+

SFX Grove currently states that there is no application or training fee. Contact us to confirm availability for the next cohort.

Do I need a Salesforce org?+

You can read SFX Forge questions without one. Signing in and connecting an org enables real execution and saved progress. Grove project requirements are explained as you enter each stage.

Is a job or paid opportunity guaranteed?+

No. There is no job guarantee. Any paid opportunity depends on visible milestone eligibility and a separate written offer. Individual learning and career outcomes vary.

Can beginners and experienced professionals join?+

The path begins with foundations and moves into integrations, delivery and AI. Your fit still depends on your current goals, available time and willingness to complete the work, so message us with your background.
