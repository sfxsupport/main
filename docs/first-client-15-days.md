# SFX: first Salesforce client campaign

Owner: Krrish Joshi. Window: 21 November–5 December 2026, India time. Goal: one signed, funded, bounded Salesforce development engagement. These are experiment targets, not a guarantee or an established conversion forecast.

The website is the destination for the offer and proof. The immediate acquisition work is warm introductions, relevant consultancy conversations, and selective marketplace proposals. Allow 60–90 minutes each working day for acquisition, plus calls and delivery. Confirm your actual delivery availability before quoting.

## What we sell first

**Salesforce Fix & Improve:** investigate one Apex or Flow problem; agree expected behaviour, scope, acceptance criteria, relevant tests, delivery owner, price, and handover. After scoping, offer a fixed-price implementation if appropriate. Where the cause or effort is uncertain, quote a bounded paid diagnostic first. Integration or LWC tasks are alternatives when they fit your stronger evidence.

Choose a task that fits your available capacity. Do not promise a turnaround before reproducing the issue and agreeing access. Price from expected effort, complexity, risk, and the agreed deliverables. Obtain a funded milestone on the marketplace, or an agreed deposit and written scope for direct work, before implementation. A small paid engagement is the initial goal; expand only after the first delivery succeeds.

## Messages ready to personalise

### Warm introduction

Hi [Name], I’m taking on small Salesforce development engagements through SFX Support, initially Apex/Flow fixes and focused integration work. You know my work on [specific shared project/context]. Do you know a Salesforce team or consultancy with a bounded backlog item I could help deliver? Here’s a relevant example: https://sfxsupport.com/work/sfx-forge/. I can scope the task before we commit.

### Consultancy approach

Hi [Name], I saw [specific service or project on their own website]. I’m Krrish, founder of SFX Support, and I’m available to discuss a bounded Apex/Flow or integration backlog item. I built [precise part of Forge you actually own]; here’s the project: https://sfxsupport.com/work/sfx-forge/. If your delivery team occasionally needs extra capacity, would it be useful to compare one requirement against my availability? [State genuine weekly capacity and time-zone overlap.]

### Marketplace proposal

Your requirement mentions [specific failure/desired behaviour]. I’d start by [concrete investigation], then [implementation and relevant test approach].

I built [specific relevant functionality]; [attach the matching work sample and explain your personal contribution].

One question before quoting: [the missing detail that changes scope]. If it fits, we can agree one funded milestone with written acceptance criteria, tests, and handover. I can discuss the details here on the platform.

Use the marketplace’s own messaging and contracting flow. Do not paste a generic biography, invent a diagnosis, or treat a personal product as a client reference.

### One follow-up

Hi [Name], following up on my note about [their actual need]. A small first engagement could be [relevant bounded task], with scope and acceptance criteria agreed before work starts. Is this useful for your team now, or should I close the loop?

### Discovery call: 15–20 minutes

1. What is the business impact, and what should happen instead?
2. Can you reproduce the problem? What changed recently?
3. Which objects, automations, APIs, and environments are involved?
4. Who owns access, review, deployment, and the decision to hire?
5. What budget and deadline are realistic?
6. Agree the next step: a written scope, a paid diagnostic, or no engagement.

### One-page proposal outline

Problem and expected result; scope and exclusions; named delivery owner; required inputs/access; acceptance criteria; test and review approach; delivery date after inputs are available; price and milestone/payment terms; handover and support; change request process. Use the buyer’s real requirement. A sample proposal is not a signed contract.

## Measure and adjust

Use `outreach-tracker.csv` for all outbound work. Website development enquiries appear in the existing SFX Leads inbox under **Contact page**, with form **development_enquiry**; community enquiries use **community_enquiry**. Budget and timing are included in the enquiry message. Existing contact stages remain New, Replied, and Closed; use the tracker to record qualified/call/proposal/won stages.

Tag direct outreach links, for example:

`https://sfxsupport.com/salesforce-development/?utm_source=linkedin&utm_medium=outreach&utm_campaign=first_client_15d`

Use source `referral` for warm introductions. Do not tag normal internal navigation as a new campaign. Marketplace proposals should follow the platform’s own communication requirements.

The new pages preserve attribution through internal navigation where session storage is available. Analytics records `form_start`, CTA interest, and `generate_lead` only after the API confirms success. Duplicate submissions do not create another conversion. A WhatsApp/email click is contact intent, not proof a message was sent.

Directional campaign goals: 10 warm introduction requests, 30 researched consultancy approaches, 15 high-fit marketplace proposals, 3 qualified calls, 2 scoped proposals, 1 paid engagement. These are planning goals, not expected conversion rates.

- No replies after the first 10 personalised approaches: review relevance, the person approached, proof, and the opening sentence.
- Replies but no calls: narrow the offer and make the proposed next step easier to assess.
- Calls but no scope: check business urgency, budget, delivery fit, and decision-maker.
- Proposals but no win: ask for the objection. Resolve scope/trust/timing issues before changing price.
- Visits but no enquiry: inspect source quality, contact clicks, form starts, and confirmed submissions. Review actual data before changing design again.
