How it works

From problem to handover in five steps

  1. Tell us the problemUse the form or WhatsApp. A short call if needed, usually 15–20 minutes.
  2. Written scope and fixed quoteWhat we'll do, what we won't, the timeline and the price. Nothing starts until you agree.
  3. Build in your sandboxAI-assisted development, with a developer reviewing every change.
  4. Test with youWe test against the scenarios in the scope and show you the result.
  5. HandoverDeployment support and short notes on what changed and why. The code is yours.

How we use AI

AI makes us faster. A developer stays responsible.

We use AI for the slow parts of Salesforce work, so you pay for less time. It never replaces review and testing.

What AI does for us

  • Reads long debug logs and points to the likely cause
  • Drafts test classes and test data
  • Drafts handover notes and documentation
  • Checks code for governor-limit and security issues

What a developer always does

  • Understands your org before changing it
  • Reviews and tests every line before handover
  • Keeps your data out of public AI tools unless you agree
  • Works in a sandbox, not straight in production

How much does it cost?+

Every task gets a fixed quote after we understand the scope. You see the price, timeline and exclusions in writing before any work starts.

How do you get access to our org?+

We prefer to work in a sandbox with a user you create and can switch off at any time. We're happy to sign an NDA before we see anything.

Will our data go into AI tools?+

Not without your agreement. By default we use AI on code, logs with personal data removed, and our own notes, never on your customer records.

We're a Salesforce partner. Can you work under our brand?+

Yes. We can work inside your delivery process, tools and naming, and talk only to your team. We'd suggest one small paid pilot first so you can judge the quality.

What if the fix doesn't work?+

We test against the scenarios agreed in the scope before handover. If something we changed doesn't meet that scope, we fix it without extra cost.

Which time zones do you work in?+

We're based in India (IST). That overlaps with UK and Europe working hours and with US early mornings or evenings.

Are you a Salesforce partner?+

No. SFX is an independent Salesforce + AI team, not a Salesforce partner and not endorsed by Salesforce. You work directly with the founder and the developer on your project.

Are you affiliated with Salesforce?+

No. SFX Support is independent and is not a Salesforce partner. We work on your org with the access you give us.
